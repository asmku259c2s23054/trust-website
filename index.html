About Us

Rotary Galaxy Scan Foundation is a premier, Government-approved Non-Governmental organization (NGO) dedicated to bridging the gap between advanced medical diagnostics and the underserved sections of society. Established and managed by the collective strength of the Rotary Club of Udumalpet Galaxy, our foundation serves as a beacon of health and hope in the Udumalpet region.

We take pride in our commitment to transparency, accountability, and excellence in service. As a fully compliant organization, we hold 12A and 80G certifications, providing tax benefits to our generous donors. Additionally, we are registered for CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) initiatives and maintained on the NITI Aayog Darpan portal, ensuring we meet the highest standards of institutional governance.

Our foundation operates on the belief that quality healthcare is a fundamental right, not a luxury. By leveraging state-of-the-art scanning technology and the selfless spirit of Rotary, we aim to provide accurate diagnostics at accessible costs, saving lives through early detection and professional care.

Vision

To be the most trusted diagnostic foundation in the region, ensuring that no individual is deprived of high-quality medical imaging and healthcare due to financial or social barriers.

Mission

To provide advanced scanning and diagnostic services to the rural and urban poor at subsidized or no cost, while maintaining the highest clinical standards.

Partnership & Awareness

We collaborate under CSR and 80G frameworks, and conduct health camps emphasizing the importance of early diagnosis in preventive medicine.
