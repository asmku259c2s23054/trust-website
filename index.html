About Us

WHO WE ARE

Rotary Galaxy Scan Foundation is a government-approved NGO focused on bridging advanced diagnostic care and underserved communities through accessible, accountable healthcare.

SERVING WITH PURPOSE

We combine healthcare technology, social service, and partnership with donors and volunteers to ensure quality diagnostic support reaches the people who need it most.

Rotary Galaxy Scan Foundation stands at the intersection of diagnostics and public service, helping families gain access to reliable healthcare through outreach programmes, certified systems, and compassionate support.

Latest News

Official Trust Deed Registration

Rotary Galaxy Scan Foundation is officially registered under a Trust Deed, emphasizing our commitment to integrity and community service. Established by Mr. A. Nazeer Hussain, the foundation strives to provide accessible healthcare solutions to the people.

Approved for CSR Activities

We are proud to announce that our foundation has been officially registered and approved by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs for undertaking Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) activities. We invite corporate partners to join us in our mission.

Section 12A Provisional Registration

Rotary Galaxy Scan Foundation has successfully received its provisional registration under Section 12A of the Income Tax Act. This certification reinforces our charitable nature and transparency in all our healthcare initiatives.

Section 80G Provisional Approval

We are pleased to announce that Rotary Galaxy Scan Foundation has been granted provisional approval under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. This certificate enables our generous donors to claim tax deductions on their contributions towards our charitable causes.
